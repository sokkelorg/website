# Sub-processors

These sub-processors may process personal data for every customer, as described in section 3 of the [Data Processing Agreement](/terms/dpa).

- **Google Cloud EMEA Limited:** Hosting of the Sokkel platform and the Dina control plane (Google Cloud). Data stored in Finland and Sweden.
- **Google Cloud EMEA Limited:** Email and documents (Google Workspace). Data stored in the EEA and the United States.

## Partner regions

A region with "-p-" in its name runs in a data centre operated by a partner. Customers who use a partner region get a sub-processor list of their own, which names the partner, with their agreement or before they first use the region.

## Changes

We email every customer at least 30 days before we add or replace a sub-processor on their list. A customer can object within those 30 days, as described in section 3.3 of the [Data Processing Agreement](/terms/dpa).
