# General Terms

These terms apply to every subscription agreement between Sokkel AS and a customer. The subscription agreement says what the Customer buys, for how long and at what price. These terms say how the parties work together. Version 2026-10.

## 1. The agreement

1.1 **Documents.** The agreement is the subscription agreement, these General Terms, Appendix 1 (Service Levels) and Appendix 2 (Data Processing Agreement). All of them are attached to the signed subscription agreement. Nothing else is part of the agreement, including terms in a purchase order.

1.2 **If the documents disagree.** Appendix 2 decides for personal data. Otherwise the subscription agreement comes first, then these General Terms, then Appendix 1.

1.3 **Changes to these terms.** Sokkel may publish a new version of these terms. A new version applies to an existing agreement only from the next renewal, and only if Sokkel sends it to the Customer at least 30 days before the notice deadline under section 9.2. Any other change needs a written agreement signed by both parties.

## 2. The service

2.1 **The services.** Sokkel delivers the services listed in the subscription agreement ("the services") for as long as the agreement lasts. The services can be software, such as the Sokkel platform, and hosting, such as Dina: running the Customer's applications, databases, Kubernetes clusters, logs, metrics and traces.

2.2 **Improvements.** Sokkel improves the services continuously and may add, change or remove features. Sokkel will not remove a core feature of a service the Customer has ordered during a term. Sokkel may move the Customer's nodes to new hardware or to a newer node family, with at least six months' notice for a family that is retired, as long as they keep at least the same vCPUs, memory and disk at the same price or lower.

2.3 **People and care.** Sokkel provides the services with skilled people and with the care expected of a professional supplier.

2.4 **Support and service levels.** Appendix 1 describes support, how errors are fixed, how fast, and what the Customer gets if Sokkel is late.

2.5 **Where the services run.** The services run in the regions named in the subscription agreement, or that the Customer chooses in the services. Sokkel runs them on its own servers or on capacity rented from infrastructure providers. The providers in use are listed at [sokkel.io/terms/sub-processors](https://sokkel.io/terms/sub-processors).

2.6 **The Customer's own infrastructure.** If the Customer connects its own servers or cloud accounts to the services, the Customer keeps its own agreement with that provider and pays it directly. Sokkel is responsible for its own software and work on that infrastructure, not for the infrastructure itself.

## 3. Users, applications and use

3.1 **Users.** The Customer's employees and contractors may use the services ("Users"). Other companies in the Customer's group may use it only if the subscription agreement says so. The Customer is responsible for what its Users do in the services.

3.2 **Accounts.** Each login belongs to one person and must not be shared. The Customer tells Sokkel straight away if it suspects that an account has been misused.

3.3 **What the Customer must not do.** The Customer must not resell the services unless the subscription agreement allows it, give access to anyone who is not a User, try to copy or take apart Sokkel's code, use the services to break the law, send spam or attack other systems, or use the services in a way that harms their availability or security for others.

3.4 **Integrations.** Some features connect to other companies' services, such as chat tools or language models. Sokkel cannot promise that those services keep working with Sokkel.

3.5 **The Customer's applications.** The Customer decides what it runs and stores on the services, and is responsible for its applications, their content and the licences they need. Sokkel does not monitor that content.

3.6 **Illegal content.** If Sokkel learns that the Customer stores or spreads clearly illegal content through the services, Sokkel may block access to that content straight away. Sokkel tells the Customer what it blocked and why.

## 4. The Customer's data

4.1 **Ownership.** The Customer owns all data it or its applications put into the services, and all results the services produce from that data ("Customer Data"). Sokkel may use Customer Data only to provide the services to the Customer, and never sells it or holds it back.

4.2 **Export.** The Customer can get all Customer Data at any time while the agreement lasts and for 30 days after it ends, in standard formats: database dumps, the files in object storage, container images and configuration, and CSV or JSON for everything else.

4.3 **Deletion.** Sokkel deletes Customer Data within 60 days after the agreement ends, including backups, unless the law requires Sokkel to keep it.

4.4 **Personal data.** Sokkel processes personal data in Customer Data on the Customer's behalf, as described in Appendix 2.

## 5. Security

5.1 **Measures.** Sokkel protects the services and Customer Data with the measures in section 6 of Appendix 2, and keeps them at least at that level for as long as the agreement lasts.

5.2 **Incidents.** If Customer Data is lost or exposed, Sokkel tells the Customer within 24 hours of finding out, and restores lost data from backup without extra charge.

## 6. Fees and payment

6.1 **Fees.** The fees are in the subscription agreement. Services the Customer uses that are not in the subscription agreement are charged at the price list for the region they run in, on the day they are used. Dina's price lists are at [dina.sh/pricing](https://dina.sh/pricing). Sokkel announces increases in the price list by email at least 30 days before they apply. Prices exclude VAT.

6.2 **Invoices.** Fixed fees are invoiced in advance for each billing period. Usage, such as Users, storage or compute, is measured by Sokkel, shown to the Customer in the services, and invoiced after each month.

6.3 **Disputed invoices.** If the Customer disagrees with an invoice, it tells Sokkel in writing before the due date and pays the part it agrees with. The rest is due when the disagreement is settled.

6.4 **Late payment.** If the Customer pays late, Sokkel may charge late payment interest at the rate set under the Norwegian Late Payment Interest Act (forsinkelsesrenteloven).

6.5 **Price changes.** Prices are fixed for the whole term. For a renewal, Sokkel may change prices by telling the Customer in writing at least 30 days before the notice deadline under section 9.2. The Customer then has time to decide whether to renew.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 **What is confidential.** Each party keeps the other party's confidential information secret and uses it only for this agreement. Customer Data and the terms of this agreement are always confidential. Information that is public, that a party already had, or that it gets lawfully from someone else is not confidential.

7.2 **When disclosure is allowed.** A party may disclose confidential information when the law requires it, including under the Norwegian Freedom of Information Act (offentleglova). If it can, it tells the other party first.

7.3 **How long.** This duty lasts for five years after the agreement ends. For Customer Data, it lasts for as long as Sokkel holds the data.

7.4 **References.** Sokkel may name the Customer as a customer only with the Customer's written consent.

## 8. Rights to the service

8.1 **Sokkel's rights.** Sokkel owns its software and everything in the services, except Customer Data and the Customer's applications. The agreement gives the Customer a right to use the services, not ownership of them.

8.2 **Claims from others.** If someone claims that the Customer's use of the services infringes their rights, Sokkel handles the claim at its own cost and covers what the Customer must pay as a result. The Customer tells Sokkel about the claim promptly, lets Sokkel lead the defence and helps where reasonable. Sokkel may instead get the right for the Customer to keep using the services, or change them so that they no longer infringe. If neither is possible, either party may end the agreement and Sokkel refunds fees paid for the time after it ends.

8.3 **Claims about the Customer's content.** If someone claims that Customer Data or the Customer's applications infringe their rights, the Customer handles the claim on the same terms as in section 8.2.

## 9. Term, renewal and ending

9.1 **Term.** The agreement starts on the start date and lasts for the initial term in the subscription agreement.

9.2 **Renewal.** At the end of each term, the agreement renews for another term of the same length. Either party can stop the renewal by telling the other in writing before the notice deadline in the subscription agreement.

9.3 **Ending for breach.** If a party materially breaches the agreement, the other party may end it by written notice, if the breach is not fixed within 30 days after it was described in writing. A category A error lasting more than 10 days, or a category B error lasting more than 20 days, is always a material breach by Sokkel (see Appendix 1).

9.4 **Insolvency.** A party may end the agreement immediately if the other party is declared bankrupt, stops paying its debts, or enters debt negotiation.

9.5 **Refunds.** If the Customer ends the agreement under section 9.3, 9.4 or 13.1, Sokkel refunds fees paid for the time after it ends. Otherwise fees are not refunded.

9.6 **After the end.** The services stop. Each party returns or deletes the other party's confidential information. The Customer can still export Customer Data under section 4.2.

## 10. Suspension

10.1 **Unpaid invoices.** If an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, Sokkel may warn the Customer in writing that the services will be suspended. If the invoice is still unpaid 14 days after the warning, Sokkel may suspend the services until it is paid. Suspension never deletes Customer Data.

10.2 **Security threats.** Sokkel may suspend a User, an application or an integration straight away if it threatens the security or availability of the services, for example because it has been taken over by an attacker. Sokkel tells the Customer why and restores access as soon as the threat is gone.

## 11. Liability

11.1 **Direct loss only.** A party that breaches the agreement covers the other party's direct loss. Neither party covers indirect loss, such as lost profit, lost savings or lost business.

11.2 **Cap.** Each party's total liability is limited to the fees the Customer paid in the 12 months before the event that caused the loss. If the agreement has lasted less than 12 months, the cap is the fees paid so far, scaled up to 12 months.

11.3 **Outside the cap.** The cap does not apply to loss caused by gross negligence or intent, to claims under sections 8.2 and 8.3, or to the Customer's duty to pay fees.

11.4 **Fines.** Each party pays its own fines under the GDPR (Article 83) and cannot claim them from the other party.

11.5 **Fix first.** Before claiming compensation for an error, the Customer gives Sokkel the chance to fix it as described in Appendix 1. Free days under Appendix 1 are deducted from any compensation for the same error.

## 12. Subcontractors and assignment

12.1 **Subcontractors.** Sokkel may use subcontractors. Sokkel is responsible for their work as if it were its own. Subcontractors that process personal data are listed in Appendix 2.

12.2 **Assignment.** Neither party may transfer the agreement without the other party's written consent, which cannot be refused without good reason. Consent is not needed when a party transfers it as part of a merger or a sale of all or most of its business, if it tells the other party in writing beforehand.

## 13. Events outside a party's control

13.1 **Force majeure.** A party is not responsible for failing to perform while it is prevented by something outside its control that it could not reasonably have foreseen or avoided, such as war, natural disasters, strikes or a widespread failure of the internet. It tells the other party as soon as it can. If the situation lasts more than 90 days, either party may end the agreement with 15 days' written notice.

## 14. Notices and disputes

14.1 **Notices.** Notices are sent by email to the contact persons in the subscription agreement. An email counts as received on the next business day in Norway. Each party keeps its contact details up to date.

14.2 **Law.** Norwegian law governs the agreement.

14.3 **Disputes.** The parties first try to settle a dispute by negotiation. If they have not settled it 30 days after one party asked for negotiation in writing, either party may take it to Oslo District Court (Oslo tingrett), which the parties agree is the court for disputes under the agreement.
